Juliana Mothersbaugh is a PhD student in political science at Stanford University and former pre-doctoral fellow at Yale University. Her research interests center how individuals contact, experience, and are affected by American civil and criminal legal institutions, as well as the role of legal institutions in reducing or expanding (in)equality and democracy more broadly.
