Hunter Rendleman is an assistant professor of political science at UC Berkeley. Her research agenda examines political and politicized workplaces, studying how identity and ideology shape who enters them, how people work, and what they can achieve together. Dr. Rendleman’s recent work examines how crisis reshapes government talent and if political shocks alter bureaucratic behavior.
